Day by day

  2. Day 2Las Vegas → Zion · 265 km

ORDItinerary 02

Historic Route 66

Duration
16 days · 15 nights
Start / end
Chicago → Los Angeles
Distance
about 4,000 km
Format
Self-drive or group
  1. Chicago
  2. St. Louis
  3. Springfield
  4. Oklahoma City
  5. Amarillo
  6. Albuquerque
  7. Holbrook
  8. Grand Canyon
  9. Las Vegas
  10. Los Angeles
Day by day
  1. Day 1–2Chicago

    The Windy City, and the corner of Adams and Michigan where the road begins.

  2. Day 3Chicago → St. Louis · 480 km

    Prairies, the Gemini Giant and the Gateway Arch.

  3. Day 4St. Louis → Springfield · 424 km

    The Ozark Highlands and the Meramec Caverns.

  4. Day 5Springfield → Oklahoma City · 460 km

    Kansas, Tulsa and the Blue Whale of Catoosa.

  5. Day 6Oklahoma City → Amarillo · 464 km

    The Route 66 Museum and the plains of the Texas Panhandle.

  6. Day 7Amarillo → Albuquerque · 460 km

    Cadillac Ranch, the MidPoint Café, then New Mexico.

  7. Day 8–9Albuquerque · Santa Fe

    Old Town, the Pueblo Cultural Center and a day among the galleries of Santa Fe.

  8. Day 10Albuquerque → Holbrook · 390 km

    Trading posts, Petrified Forest and sunset over the Painted Desert.

  9. Day 11Holbrook → Grand Canyon · 390 km

    Flagstaff, Williams and the South Rim.

  10. Day 12Williams → Las Vegas · 352 km

    The longest unbroken stretch of the old road, Seligman and the Hoover Dam.

  11. Day 13Las Vegas

    A free day.

  12. Day 14Las Vegas → Santa Monica

    Calico ghost town, Barstow and the pier where Route 66 ends.

  13. Day 15Los Angeles

    A day to explore the city.

  14. Day 16Los Angeles

    Departure.

  3. Day 3Everglades

    The Gulf coast and its sunsets.

  5. Day 5Sanibel · Captiva → Sarasota

    Fort Myers and the shell beaches of Sanibel and Captiva.

  6. Day 6Crystal River · Homosassa

    Clear springs where the manatees spend the winter.

  7. Day 7Crystal River → St. Augustine

    The oldest city in the United States, founded in 1565.

  8. Day 8–10Orlando

    Three days for the theme parks.

  9. Day 11Cape Canaveral · Cocoa Beach

    Kennedy Space Center, then the Atlantic.

  10. Day 12Miami

    Back to Miami and departure.

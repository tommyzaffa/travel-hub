Sample itinerary 01

Great Parks of the West

Duration
10 days · 9 nights
Start / end
Las Vegas
Distance
about 1,840 km
States
Nevada · Utah · Arizona
  1. Las Vegas
  2. Zion
  3. Bryce Canyon
  4. Page
  5. Grand Canyon
  6. Sedona
  7. Lake Havasu
  8. Las Vegas
Day by day
  1. Day 1Las Vegas

    Arrival, transfer to the hotel and a first evening on the Strip.

  2. Day 2Las Vegas → Zion · 265 km

    Across the Nevada desert to Zion, a canyon 24 km long and 800 metres deep.

  3. Day 3Zion → Bryce Canyon · 135 km

    A short drive over the Utah plateau to the red hoodoos of Bryce. Sunrise is worth the early start.

  4. Day 4Bryce Canyon → Page · 240 km

    Lake Powell, Horseshoe Bend and a walk through Antelope Canyon.

  5. Day 5Page → Grand Canyon · 340 km

    On to the South Rim of the Grand Canyon.

  6. Day 6Grand Canyon

    A full day in the park, with an optional helicopter flight.

  7. Day 7Grand Canyon → Sedona · 205 km

    Red rocks and the slower pace of Sedona.

  8. Day 8Sedona → Lake Havasu · 410 km

    South-west to Lake Havasu, between sun and water.

  9. Day 9Lake Havasu → Las Vegas · 245 km

    Back to Las Vegas to close the loop.

  10. Day 10Las Vegas

    Departure.

Sample itinerary 02

New England: lighthouses & lobsters

Duration
13 days · 12 nights
Start / end
Boston
Format
Group or self-drive
States
MA · ME · NH · VT · CT · RI
  1. Boston
  2. Kennebunkport
  3. Camden
  4. Bar Harbor
  5. Bethel
  6. North Conway
  7. Londonderry
  8. Stockbridge
  9. Mystic
  10. Cape Cod
  11. Boston
Day by day
  1. Day 1–2Boston

    The Freedom Trail, Harvard and Cambridge, Faneuil Hall, and a free day in the city.

  2. Day 3Boston → Kennebunkport

    North along the coast via Salem, Rockport, Portsmouth and Ogunquit.

  3. Day 4Kennebunkport → Camden

    Portland Head Light, Portland’s Old Port and Pemaquid Point.

  4. Day 5Camden → Bar Harbor

    Along Penobscot Bay to Mount Desert Island.

  5. Day 6Bar Harbor → Bethel

    Morning in Acadia National Park and on Cadillac Mountain, then inland.

  6. Day 7Bethel → North Conway

    The White Mountains and Mount Washington.

  7. Day 8North Conway → Londonderry

    Kancamagus Highway, covered bridges, Franconia Notch and Woodstock, Vermont.

  8. Day 9Londonderry → Stockbridge

    The Green Mountains, Bennington and the Norman Rockwell Museum.

  9. Day 10Stockbridge → Mystic

    Down to the Connecticut coast and Mystic Seaport.

  10. Day 11Mystic → Cape Cod

    The Gilded Age mansions of Newport on the way to Cape Cod.

  11. Day 12Cape Cod → Boston

    Provincetown and the villages of the Cape, Plymouth, back to Boston.

  12. Day 13Boston

    Departure.

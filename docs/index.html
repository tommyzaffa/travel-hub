How we work

Four steps, one contact

The same person follows your file from the brief to the return flight.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You tell us who’s travelling, when, for how long and with what budget.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    We send a detailed itinerary with costs, hotels and options, and refine it with you until it’s right.

  3. 03

    Operations

    We book and confirm every service and prepare the digital travel kit for you and your travellers.

  4. 04

    On the ground

    During the trip we stay reachable and solve problems before they become yours.
